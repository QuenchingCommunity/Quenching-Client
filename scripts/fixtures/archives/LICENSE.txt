compressed-rar5.base64 is a base64 encoding of libarchive's
libarchive/test/test_read_format_rar5_compressed.rar.uu fixture.
Source: https://github.com/libarchive/libarchive/blob/master/libarchive/test/test_read_format_rar5_compressed.rar.uu
The fixture was obtained on 2026-10-26. It expands to test.bin (1200 bytes,
CRC32 7CCA70CD). It is used only by the development tests and is not shipped.
